# How to make waffles
This article shows you how to make waffles.
## Steps:
1. Get out a mixing bowl, whisk, and wafflemaker.
2. Gather 1 cup of pancake/waffle mix, 1/4 cup of milk, 1 egg, and 4 tbsp of softened butter.
3. Mix the ingredients into a bowl.
4. Turn on the waffle maker and wait until the light turns green.
5. Pour the batter into the wafflemaker.
6. Close the lid and flip the wafflemaker.
7. Take the waffles out when the wafflemaker starts steaming or the light turns green.
8. *Optional*: Serve with maple syrup and butter.
